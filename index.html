SLIPDAW

A full digital audio workstation that runs entirely in this tab — channel rack, piano roll, playlist, mixer and mastering chain, with five built-in instruments and seventeen effect plugins. Nothing is uploaded and nothing is installed.

A note on plugins: real VST/VST3 files are compiled native binaries and cannot run in a browser — no site can load them. Every instrument and effect here is written in Web Audio instead, so they do the same job without leaving the page.

Best on a desktop with headphones. A demo song loads automatically — press play.
